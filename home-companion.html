Before a physical product

The important questions come first

A companion in a shared home would need more than a friendly form. It would need evidence, clear ownership, and trustworthy limits.

01

Consent at home

How should household members, visitors, and minors understand and control any future sensing or data use?

02

Physical safety and control

What local stops, fail-safe behavior, motion limits, and visible indicators would be required before hardware work?

03

Trust over time

How would secure ownership transfer, updates, privacy choices, support, and eventual retirement remain understandable?
