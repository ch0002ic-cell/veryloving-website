A future companion for the home

VeryLoving is exploring how a future home product could offer warmth and companionship. These are research directions, not working product features.

A separate long-term roadmap

The current experience is a static research surface

No physical Home Companion is connected. Nothing on this page controls, monitors, listens through, senses for, or sends commands to a device.

The goal is to discuss warmth, utility, privacy, and healthy boundaries before product capabilities or hardware are designed.

Explore a calm, welcoming presence for adults at home—designed as a social research direction, not as monitoring, elder care, therapy, or emergency support.

Supportive routines

Choice before automation

Study reminders and household routines that begin with clear user choice. No device, service integration, sensor, or autonomous behavior exists today.

Human
connection

Natural interaction questions

Personalization needs consent

User-chosen visual styles, friendly expressions, and conversational cues are research questions—not current features. Any future likeness would require consent and appropriate rights. The concept does not recognize emotions; it could only respond to what a person intentionally chooses to share.

Privacy + emotional boundaries

Trust must be designed before a device

A companion in a shared home would need evidence, clear ownership, and understandable limits.

Consent at home. Household members, visitors, and minors would need understandable choices.

No emotion detection. The current research page does not monitor feelings, rooms, or people.

Healthy attachment. A future concept must not replace people, caregivers, therapy, or emergency help.

Trust over time. Ownership transfer, updates, privacy choices, support, and retirement would need proof.
