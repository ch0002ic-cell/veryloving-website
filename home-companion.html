Long-term vision, honest current boundary

Technology that feels more human

Home Companion is an early research concept and is not available today.

We’re exploring what a calm, friendly presence could mean for people who want more warmth and connection at home. The current implementation is a loopback-only, process-memory simulator—not a physical robot or a production service.

Warm
presence

Mobile-orchestrated continuity

A gentle check-in, requested on purpose

A developer explicitly starts the rehearsal from the mobile app after both localhost endpoints are connected. Home Companion accepts only the closed gentle-check-in payload. Its correlation identifier follows the local intent, no-capture session, receipts, and events; a wearable has no direct authority over the Home endpoint.

Opt in
first

No-capture session

Helpful, always on your terms

The synthetic session requests no capture, exposes no camera, and never accesses a browser microphone. Capture remains prohibited and inactive throughout the rehearsal, including its bounded handoff lease.

Human
connection

Receipts without assumptions

Evidence stays precise

Each explicit advance creates one synthetic receipt, keeping transport acknowledgement, acceptance, execution, and terminal evidence distinct. The mobile app reports each endpoint's outcome and treats the rehearsal as completed only after both report executed and it explicitly ends the correlated Home session. A lost terminal receipt becomes unknown—not success.

Designed around trust

Respect belongs at the heart of the home

The local rehearsal is deliberately user-started, no-capture, effect-free, and fail-closed.

Explicit control.The mobile developer starts one correlated rehearsal, manually refreshes its evidence, and explicitly advances or ends its synthetic session.

No capture.No camera, microphone capture, audio provider, conversation, monitoring, notification, or location flow is involved.

Local readiness.Ready, busy, or blocked describes only the process-memory harness—not a person, home, robot, or production system.

Fail closed.Privacy or availability faults, lease reconciliation, rebind, and reset end or block work without silently resuming it.

Built
with care

Production gates remain closed

From idea to home, one verified step at a time

The negative-control production-gate mode rejects every semantic request and remains synthetic. Product device identity, secure remote transport, household consent policy, physical privacy evidence, hardware validation, and release approval all remain future work.

Follow the journey

A careful path from prototype to home

Current receipts prove only local software behavior. A future product would require physical-device, privacy, safety, security, accessibility, human-factors, operational, and regulatory validation.
