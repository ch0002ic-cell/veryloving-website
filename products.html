About VeryLoving

Personal safety, with a more human touch

VeryLoving is a Silicon Valley–based AI safety technology company dedicated to advancing personal safety, AI-powered emotional companionship, and next-generation wearable intelligence.

Our mission is simple: to make the world safer—and more compassionate.

Today’s coordinated prototype is an engineering rehearsal: a developer explicitly asks the iOS or Android app to record one synthetic check-in cue with two same-computer simulators. It does not connect physical jewelry or a robot, monitor anyone, or create an external effect.

Prototype 1.4 is an internal software-contract milestone, not an app release or product version. Each simulator self-reports the same closed rehearsal catalog, and the mobile lab checks that evidence before a developer can explicitly run one of three stepwise plans: ordinary completion, queued cancellation, or transport uncertainty. A passing plan means only that the expected local synthetic contract evidence was observed; it does not establish hardware, delivery, monitoring, AI safety decisions, or production readiness.

Coordinated rehearsal

A developer chooses one named plan. The mobile lab advances it through discrete local calls. The ordinary-completion plan passes only after both local intents reach executed and the app explicitly ends the matching Home session. Cancellation and transport uncertainty stay distinct and are never relabeled as completion.

Evidence review

A developer can manually refresh the original endpoint records and inspect a separate timeline of at most 32 redacted, in-memory entries. The timeline omits tokens, URLs, endpoint, binding, request, intent, session, and correlation identifiers, timestamps, raw payloads, trace detail, and user, location, audio, or device data, and clears when either endpoint disconnects or the lab is backgrounded or left.

Production path

Physical hardware, secure transport, consent controls, validation, operations, and release approvals remain separate production gates.
