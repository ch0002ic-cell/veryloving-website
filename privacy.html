Privacy notice

This candidate notice describes the data practices being evaluated for the VeryLoving mobile and wearable companion concept.

Last updated: 17 Aug 2026

Status: This notice is a candidate under product, privacy, security, and legal review. Publishing it does not mean that an app build, capability, or store submission has been approved. It must be updated to match the exact data practices of any reviewed release before distribution.

Who is responsible

veryloving Inc. is responsible for the VeryLoving experience described on this page. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to george@verylovinginc.com.

Scope of this candidate notice

VeryLoving is still under development. The categories below reflect the current candidate app design. A category is relevant only when its corresponding feature is enabled in the reviewed build and the user chooses to use it. Prototype, development, and production builds may not expose the same features.

The website files published by veryloving Inc. do not include a hosted form, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. Selecting Subscribe opens an external Google Form. An embedded YouTube video may process routine request and device information under YouTube's privacy practices, and the website host may process routine request information, such as an IP address and browser details, under its own practices. The local site script only controls the FAQ accordion and does not collect or transmit visitor data.

Data the candidate app may process

Category When it may be processed Candidate purpose
Account and authentication data When a user signs in, including the phone number or other sign-in identifier provided and account or session identifiers generated by the authentication service. Authenticate the user, maintain a signed-in session, and bind requested account activity to the correct account.
Profile content When a user chooses to add information such as a display name or profile image. Display and manage the profile the user requested.
Voice interaction data When a user deliberately starts a voice interaction, including microphone audio and the resulting interaction content needed to complete that request. Provide the user-started AI-assisted voice experience.
Location data When a location feature is enabled, the user grants the relevant device permission, and the user opens or requests that experience. Precision depends on the device setting. Render the requested map or location-based app experience.
User-selected content When a user chooses content for an enabled experience, such as an image, memory, or related text. Provide the specific experience selected by the user.
Technical and diagnostic context During app operation or error diagnosis, such as app version, operating-system information, and limited failure context. Service providers may also process network identifiers needed to deliver a request. Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

Service providers

An enabled feature may send only the information needed for the user's request to a service provider. The current provider inventory remains under review and must be verified against the final built artifact. Candidate integrations include:

  • Hume for an enabled, user-started AI-assisted voice interaction.
  • Mapbox for an enabled map or location experience requested by the user.
  • Apple or Google platform services, as applicable to the device, authentication method, and distributed app build.

No service-provider list should be treated as final until it has been checked against the exact release configuration.

Permissions and user choices

Device permissions, including microphone, location, and photo access, are controlled through the operating system. A user may deny or revoke a permission in device settings, although the related feature may then be unavailable. The app should request a permission only when it is required for the specific enabled experience.

Retention, account deletion, and other requests

No universal fixed retention period is approved in this candidate notice. Retention depends on the data category, the requested feature, operational and legal needs, and verified provider settings. Final release documentation must state any applicable periods once they have been confirmed.

The candidate account-deletion flow submits an authenticated deletion request and must not report completion unless the service confirms it. Limited records may remain for a period in backups, security logs, or records that must be preserved for legal reasons. To ask about access, correction, or deletion, email george@verylovinginc.com. Identity verification may be required before a request is acted on. Available request mechanisms will be documented before a reviewed release.

Security

Supported network requests in the candidate app are configured to use HTTPS/TLS in transit. Security controls, including provider-side and storage controls, must be verified for the exact release. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Storage safeguards remain under verification for the exact release.

Children

The VeryLoving concept is not directed to children under 13. Age eligibility and any additional protections must be confirmed before a reviewed release. If you believe information associated with a child has been provided, contact veryloving Inc. using the email above.

Safety boundary

VeryLoving is not an emergency service. App data, AI-assisted output, location information, and device behavior do not guarantee monitoring, message delivery, emergency response, or a safety outcome.

Changes to this notice

This page will be revised when the reviewed data practices change. The date at the top will identify the current published candidate. A final notice must reflect the exact app build made available to users.
