Privacy notice

This release-candidate notice describes the current website and the data practices observed in the reviewed VeryLoving mobile app source. It is not yet an effective production privacy policy.

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Status — release candidate, not yet effective: This notice remains under Product, Backend, Privacy, Security, and Legal review. It is not bound to an approved production app version or build. Publishing this candidate does not approve an app capability, hardware product, store submission, or United States launch. The exact release, providers, retention rules, request procedures, and security evidence must be verified before distribution. This candidate is not an approved Privacy Policy or store-release approval.

Reviewed source scope: the current draft mobile, wearable-simulator, and Home Companion simulator implementations, including the software-only prototype 1.3 boundary work described below. This scope is not an app version or release approval; any source or deployment change requires a new review.

Important current boundaries

  • Operating-system push and local notifications are not enabled in the reviewed current app source. The shipping client does not acquire a push token or schedule local notifications.
  • A user-requested phone sign-in or account-verification flow may send a one-time passcode by SMS through Twilio. No current safety, trusted-contact, or marketing flow automatically sends an SMS, places a phone call, contacts emergency services, notifies a friend or trusted contact, or shares a user's location.
  • The reviewed wearable integration does not implement physiological heart-rate or heartbeat sensing. Wearable and Home Companion localhost simulators use synthetic, process-memory data and do not establish a physical device, monitoring, or external effect.
  • The debug-only mobile continuity rehearsal starts only after a developer connects both same-computer simulators and presses its request control. iOS Simulator uses loopback; Android Emulator uses its fixed host alias. Physical phones, LAN endpoints, direct wearable-to-Home authority, and automatic activation are not supported.
  • The app presents the wearable and Home outcomes separately. It marks a rehearsal completed only when both local intents report executed and the app explicitly ends the correlated Home session. Unknown, failed, expired, cancelled, or inconsistent evidence is not treated as success.
  • Selected developer paths now bound and validate incoming live-voice frames, mapping responses, queued Bluetooth observations, outbound batches, and relevant provider replies. Malformed or oversized input is rejected, queue overflow is recorded instead of silently replacing older work, and identifier-bearing Bluetooth diagnostics are redacted. These safeguards do not establish provider or production readiness.
  • The wearable simulator adds bounded synthetic ownership, reboot, replay, and cancellation evidence. These fixtures do not establish physical identity, secure ownership, provisioning, durable storage, delivery, or hardware behavior.
  • The Home Companion integration harness accepts a no-capture check-in fixture only. It does not expose a camera or access a browser microphone, and its session always reports capture as prohibited and inactive. It does not call an AI or voice provider. Closed machine-readable schemas, a bounded allowlisted trace, and coordinated stop outcomes describe only local software evidence.
  • The current client does not provide automatic danger, distress, or emotion-based safety classification, continuous background danger or safety monitoring, emergency dispatch, or a guaranteed safety outcome.

veryloving Inc. is responsible for the VeryLoving experience described on this candidate page. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to george@verylovinginc.com. The company identity, mailing address, and privacy-request process must be confirmed in the final approved notice.

Scope of this candidate notice

This candidate covers the static VeryLoving website and data flows observed in the reviewed mobile client source as of the date above. It does not prove how an unreviewed backend, provider account, firmware image, physical product, support system, or future release handles data. A category applies only when the corresponding feature is enabled in the exact build and the user chooses to use it. Offline-demo, internal-prototype, development, and production-channel configurations expose different capabilities.

Website data practices

The website files published by veryloving Inc. do not include a hosted form, advertising pixel, or analytics script. The local site script controls the FAQ accordion and does not submit visitor data. GitHub Pages, as the current host, may receive routine request data such as an IP address, browser details, and requested page.

  • Selecting Subscribe opens an external Google Form. Google and veryloving Inc. may receive information the visitor chooses to submit there.
  • Selecting a pre-order control opens a Stripe-hosted checkout. Stripe and veryloving Inc. may receive checkout, contact, payment, fraud-prevention, and order information according to the checkout flow and their respective practices.
  • The privacy-enhanced YouTube embed and links to LinkedIn, Instagram, and X are third-party services. Opening or interacting with them may allow those services to process routine device, request, account, or interaction information under their own policies.

Mobile app data categories

Category When it may be processed Candidate purpose
Account and authentication data When a user signs in, including a phone number and verification code, or the identity tokens and available name or email sent through an Apple or Google sign-in flow. The services also generate account and session identifiers. Authenticate the user, maintain a signed-in session, and bind requested account activity to the correct account.
Profile content When a user chooses to add or change information such as a display name, profile image, language, voice preference, or app settings. Display and manage the profile the user requested.
AI voice interaction data When a signed-in user deliberately starts a live voice interaction. Hume may receive typed text and microphone audio while the user is unmuted and may return transcripts, assistant audio, and voice-signal labels. The current client discards provider labels as safety facts and keeps its display transcript only in process memory. A voice-preview request sends bundled sample text and the selected voice identifier and configuration, not microphone audio or user-entered text. Provider and backend retention, training, and deletion settings remain unverified. Provide the user-started AI-assisted voice experience.
Foreground location and map data When the user opens an enabled map or location experience and grants foreground permission. While the map is focused and the app is active, the client may watch foreground location for the requested display. Mapbox may receive search text, foreground search-proximity coordinates rounded to four decimal places, or exact origin and destination coordinates for a requested route. These can constitute precise location. The current client does not request background location permission. Render the requested map, search, route preview, or location experience.
User content and map memories When an authenticated user chooses to create an enabled map memory, including an image, note, emoji or type, exact coordinates, timestamps, and visibility selection. The reviewed live write flow currently forces self-only visibility. Create, display, synchronize, update, or delete the requested content.
Offline demo fixtures In the offline demo, fictional danger-zone fixtures may be stored on the device with synthetic coordinates, category, level, description, radius, visibility, and recipient identifiers. The current live danger-report service fails closed and sends no report. Demonstrate a local interface without representing verified safety data.
Friends and social-connection data When an authenticated user searches for another user or manages a friend request, including user identifiers, display names, profile images, relationship state, and request counts. Provide the requested social connection. A friend is not an emergency contact and does not receive monitoring or location access from friendship alone.
User-directed location sharing Only after the user presses Share, the app obtains a fresh foreground location and prepares exact coordinates, capture time, and a map link for the device's operating-system share sheet. The user chooses whether and where to send it. Complete the user's explicit sharing request. Opening the share sheet is not proof that a recipient received the data.
Local integration and continuity-rehearsal data Only in the developer-enabled lab, this may include synthetic endpoint, binding, profile, revision, request-linking, simulated observation, readiness, fault, intent, session, and receipt values. Wearable evidence can include bounded synthetic ownership, reboot, replay, and cancellation outcomes. Home evidence can include a schema-checked, bounded, allowlisted status trace and coordinated stop outcomes. The mobile app can manually refresh and reconcile each endpoint; one endpoint succeeding does not establish the other succeeded. Bounded duplicate-request tombstones can remain in wearable process memory through local reset until explicit rebind. Rehearse and diagnose bounded local software contracts without representing hardware telemetry, physical execution, human receipt, monitoring, or a safety outcome.
Technical and diagnostic context During app operation or error diagnosis, such as app version, operating-system information, and limited failure context. Service providers may also process network identifiers needed to deliver a request. Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app.
On-device data The current client uses device-protected storage for account credentials and local app storage for account-scoped friends; memory records that can include notes, image locations, and exact coordinates; map preferences; paired wearable owner, device name, identifier, and pairing time; selected settings; and bounded queued wearable events where that flow is enabled. Local-simulator bearer tokens and correlated rehearsal state are held only in app process memory and are cleared when the lab disconnects, leaves the screen, or moves out of the active state. Maintain the requested session and app state, support limited offline behavior, and retry eligible development events.

Notifications, in-app notices, and communications

The word “notification” can describe different mechanisms. In the reviewed current app, their status is:

Mechanism Current status What the user should understand
In-app popups and system dialogs Used for account or permission errors, feature limitations, development or simulation boundaries, device state, AI voice limitations, map or route errors, and destructive confirmations. These messages explain current app state or request a decision. They are not evidence that an external alert, safety action, or message was delivered.
Push and local notifications Disabled in the reviewed current app source. The client does not currently register a push token, schedule a local notification, or present a production push program.
SMS, automated calls, and email When the user requests phone authentication, Twilio may send a one-time passcode by SMS to the number provided. No current safety, trusted-contact, or marketing flow automatically sends an SMS, places a call, sends email, or contacts emergency services. Any future external-messaging feature requires a reviewed recipient-consent, content, delivery, revocation, privacy, and legal contract before activation.
Operating-system share sheet Opened only after a user explicitly requests Quick Share. The user selects the destination. The chosen app or recipient then handles the shared content under its own practices.

This durable notice does not replace a clear disclosure or consent at the moment a permission, sensitive-data flow, safety limitation, or future communication channel becomes relevant. A material limitation must not be hidden solely in a legal document.

Service providers

An enabled feature may send information to a service provider to perform the user's request or operate that feature. The exact fields, provider account settings, and current provider inventory remain under review and must be verified against the final built artifact. Candidate integrations include:

  • Hume for an enabled, user-started AI-assisted voice interaction.
  • Mapbox for an enabled map or location experience requested by the user.
  • The wearable and Home Companion localhost simulators are not service providers: they are same-computer development processes with no outbound network, persistence, physical device, or external effect.
  • Apple or Google platform services, as applicable to the device, authentication method, and distributed app build.
  • Twilio for a user-requested phone authentication or account-verification SMS in a supported live flow.
  • GitHub Pages, Google Forms, YouTube, Stripe, and linked social services for the website interactions described above.

The VeryLoving backend, database, hosting, logging, support, and provider-account configurations have not yet been verified as a complete release inventory. No service-provider list should be treated as final until it has been checked against the exact built artifact, deployed services, account settings, contracts, and subprocessors.

Advertising, analytics, sale, and targeted advertising

No dedicated advertising or product-analytics integration was found in the reviewed current mobile source, and the static website does not include an advertising pixel or analytics script. Mapbox and other providers may still process operational or provider telemetry. This source-code observation does not establish the practices of every backend, provider, embedded service, checkout, form, or linked site. A final notice must state whether personal information is sold, shared for cross-context behavioral advertising, or used for targeted advertising, and must provide any legally required opt-out or Global Privacy Control mechanism after the company-wide inventory is verified.

Permissions and user choices

Device permissions, including microphone, foreground location, Bluetooth, camera, photo-library access, and Apple or Google sign-in, are controlled through the operating system or the corresponding provider. A user may deny or revoke a permission in device settings, although the related feature may then be unavailable. The app should request a permission only when it is required for the specific enabled experience. The Home Companion localhost harness does not request camera or browser-microphone access, and its Guardian check-in fixture requires no capture. Push permission is not currently requested by the reviewed shipping flow.

Sensitive data and automated inferences

Precise location, microphone audio, voice interaction content, and data that may reveal or infer health or emotional information require heightened review. The current client does not turn Hume voice-signal labels into a distress or safety decision, but Hume's processing and retention settings remain to be verified. The exact facts must be evaluated for United States state privacy, consumer-health-data, biometric, recording, and breach-notification requirements before a relevant production feature is enabled. Broad acceptance of a legal document is not a substitute for any separate consent required by law.

Retention, account deletion, and other requests

No universal fixed retention period is approved in this candidate notice. Retention depends on the data category, the requested feature, operational and legal needs, and verified provider settings. Final release documentation must state any applicable periods once they have been confirmed.

The candidate account-deletion flow submits an authenticated deletion request and must not report completion unless the service confirms it. Limited records may remain for a period in backups, security logs, or records that must be preserved for legal reasons. To ask about access, correction, or deletion, email george@verylovinginc.com. Identity verification may be required before a request is acted on. Available request mechanisms will be documented before a reviewed release.

Depending on applicable law and the verified release, a person may have rights to ask about, access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to withdraw or appeal certain choices. California and other state laws may provide additional rights, including rights concerning sensitive information or sale and sharing. The company's legal coverage, verification method, authorized-agent process, appeal path, and response procedures remain to be approved. Exercising a legally available privacy right will not result in unlawful discrimination.

Security

Supported network requests in the candidate app are configured to use HTTPS/TLS in transit. Security controls, including provider-side and storage controls, must be verified for the exact release. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Storage safeguards remain under verification for the exact release.

The developer-only integration is a deliberate exception: it uses loopback HTTP on the same computer, with a new bearer token generated for each simulator process. The token is manually supplied to the app, kept in memory, and is not product authentication or user identity. The services remain bound to loopback, accept only fixed emulator Host values, and must not be exposed through a LAN, tunnel, or public proxy. No sensitive or production information should be entered into the lab.

Selected mobile inputs and responses are size-bounded and shape-checked before use; malformed or oversized data fails closed. Development Bluetooth diagnostics redact device identifiers where recorded. The Home trace includes only allowlisted status evidence and excludes payloads, credentials, device details, contacts, location, and audio. These controls are implementation evidence, not a security certification.

Children

The VeryLoving concept is not directed to children under 13. Age eligibility, an enforceable age-screening approach, and protections for minors have not been approved for production. The current app source does not establish a verified parental-consent workflow. If you believe information associated with a child has been provided, contact veryloving Inc. using the email above.

Safety boundary

VeryLoving is not an emergency service. App data, AI-assisted output, map or location information, a popup, a simulated observation, and device behavior do not guarantee monitoring, message delivery, emergency response, or a safety outcome. If someone may be in danger, contact 911 or the appropriate local emergency service directly.
