NorthStar wearable + mobile

A wearable companion, led by your choices

NorthStar is an engineering-stage Bluetooth wearable integration designed to open phone-based VeryLoving experiences through simple, intentional gestures.

See the interaction Pre-order wearable

NorthStar does not provide emergency help. Gestures only open phone screens; they do not automatically start audio, share location, or contact anyone.

Current product status

A development path, not a safety promise

Mobile engineering includes an account-scoped Bluetooth development path. Physical acceptance, background operation, authenticated hardware proof, and alert delivery are not verified.

Intentional by design

The person stays in the loop

A gesture opens a phone experience. The next meaningful action still belongs to you.

  1. 01

    Pair nearby

    With permission, the phone scans for the nearby development device and stores that exact device ID locally, scoped to the signed-in account.

  2. 02

    Tap to open

    A single tap can open the AI companion screen. You still choose when to press Start and begin a conversation.

  3. 03

    Hold to prepare sharing

    Press and hold can open Quick Share. You press Share, choose a destination in the phone’s share sheet, and remain in control.

  4. 04

    See the connection state

    The app distinguishes a live Bluetooth connection from a saved local pairing instead of treating registration as connectivity.

Opening the phone’s share sheet does not prove that another person received the location.

Everyday form

Designed to feel personal, not clinical

The wearable direction is intended to bring a small physical touchpoint to VeryLoving’s mobile companion experience. Its role is to make intentional moments easier to reach—not to replace a phone, professional care, or emergency services.

Clear boundaries

What NorthStar does not claim

No automatic or background danger, distress, fall, or emotion detection.

No phone-independent, dead-phone, or terminated-app protection.

No automatic SOS, emergency calling, contact notification, or delivery receipt.

No medical monitoring, diagnosis, or guaranteed safety outcome.
