Under development · NorthStar wearable + mobile

More than an accessory

NorthStar is an engineering-stage Bluetooth wearable direction designed to open phone-based VeryLoving experiences through simple, intentional gestures.

Pre-order wearable See how it works

Current product status

A development path, not a safety promise

Mobile engineering includes an account-scoped Bluetooth development path. Physical acceptance, background operation, authenticated hardware proof, and alert delivery are not verified.

NorthStar does not provide emergency help. Gestures only open phone screens; they do not automatically start audio, share location, or contact anyone.

Emotional connection · User-started

Meet Capybear

Capybear is a friendly companion character in supported mobile experiences. A single NorthStar tap can open the AI companion screen; you still choose when to press Start and begin a conversation.

Everyday form

Designed to feel personal, not clinical

The wearable direction is intended to bring a small physical touchpoint to VeryLoving’s mobile companion experience. Its role is to make intentional moments easier to reach—not to replace a phone, professional care, or emergency services.

Everyday and travel moments

Share only when you choose

Press and hold can open Quick Share. You press Share and select a destination in the phone’s share sheet. Opening that sheet does not prove another person received the location, and it is not automatic travel protection.

A gesture opens. You decide what happens next.

01 · Pair nearby

With permission, the phone scans and stores the exact device ID locally, scoped to the signed-in account.

02 · Tap to open

A single tap can open the AI companion screen. You separately choose when to start the conversation.

03 · Hold to prepare sharing

Press and hold can open Quick Share. You press Share and choose the destination in the phone interface.

04 · Read the connection state

The app distinguishes a live Bluetooth connection from a saved local pairing or registration.

Technical facts + clear boundaries

What NorthStar does not claim

The current development path remains phone-linked and user-directed.

No automatic or background danger, distress, fall, or emotion detection.

No phone-independent, dead-phone, or terminated-app protection.

No automatic SOS, emergency calling, contact notification, or delivery receipt.

No verified public battery, radio-range, water-resistance, or material specification.
