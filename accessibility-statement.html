Website accessibility

Accessibility statement

veryloving Inc. is working to make these four static webpages clear, navigable, and usable by a broad range of visitors.

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Scope

This statement covers the Home, FAQ, Privacy, and Accessibility pages published at the VeryLoving GitHub Pages website. The external Google subscription form, embedded YouTube player, and third-party pre-order checkout have their own accessibility behavior and are not covered by this statement.

Measures currently in place

The website currently includes:

  • semantic page landmarks and a consistent heading structure;
  • a skip link that moves keyboard focus to the main content;
  • keyboard-accessible links and custom FAQ accordion buttons with programmatic expanded states and associated answer regions;
  • visible focus indicators and adequately sized link targets;
  • text alternatives for meaningful images;
  • layouts that adapt to narrower screens and text resizing; and
  • reduced-motion and forced-colors accommodations where supported.

These measures describe the current implementation. They are not a claim that the website has completed an independent accessibility audit or achieved a particular conformance level.

Known limitations

Meaningful product and instructional artwork is rendered as raster imagery. Although text alternatives identify its purpose, they may not communicate every visual detail shown in the art. The external Google Form, YouTube player, and checkout have their own design and accessibility behavior, which veryloving Inc. does not control through these static pages. Additional issues may be identified as the website is reviewed with more browsers and assistive technology.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on these pages or need the information in another format, email george@verylovinginc.com. Please include the page address and a short description of the problem if you are able to do so.

Ongoing review

veryloving Inc. will review reported barriers and update the website as verified improvements are made. This statement will be revised when the site structure, accessibility measures, or known limitations materially change.
