Questions and answers

VeryLoving FAQ

The app and wearable remain under development. These answers describe the current concept without promising unverified release behavior.

What is VeryLoving?

VeryLoving is an early-stage wearable companion concept with a related mobile experience. The company is exploring ways to support user-directed connection, but the product is still being validated and is not an emergency or monitoring service.

How long does the wearable battery last?

A verified public battery-life specification is not available yet. Any final estimate will depend on tested hardware, software, and usage conditions and will be published only after validation.

Is the wearable water resistant?

No verified ingress-protection or water-resistance rating has been published. Do not assume the concept wearable can withstand water or submersion unless future product guidance explicitly confirms it.

Does VeryLoving track my location?

Map and location experiences in an enabled app build require device permission and deliberate user action. Availability can also depend on the device and network. They are not continuous emergency tracking and should not be relied on for a safety outcome.

Does the experience work without a phone or network?

The current mobile experiences depend on a compatible phone, and some behavior may require network access. Verified offline and standalone wearable behavior has not been finalized.

What happens if I activate something by accident?

Final wearable controls and cancellation behavior have not been published. Do not rely on the prototype for emergency response, time-critical communication, or guaranteed delivery.

Will the app require a subscription?

Final pricing, included services, and any subscription model have not been confirmed. Current development materials should not be treated as a final commercial offer.

Where will VeryLoving be available?

Final shipping regions and app availability have not been confirmed. Availability may depend on product readiness, regulatory requirements, and platform approval.

Can I pre-order the concept?

An existing pre-order checkout is available. Product specifications, availability, and timing remain subject to confirmation, so review the checkout details before proceeding.

How can I contact VeryLoving?

Email george@verylovinginc.com with product, pre-order, privacy, or accessibility questions.
